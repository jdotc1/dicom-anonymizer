# Contributing

Contribute code and synthetic reproductions only. Do not submit patient data, original identifiers, raw clinical exports, local credentials or private organization information. Generate a minimal reproduction with examples/generate_batch.py or an entirely fictional fixture.

Explain the behavior changed, why it matters, and the validation evidence available. Keep retention-policy changes explicit, preserve image/contour values and reference consistency, and distinguish tests executed from tests merely authored. Reuse valid prior evidence; do not expand into exhaustive corpus runs without an agreed scope.

By submitting a contribution, you must have the right to license it under the project's MIT license. Preserve all required third-party copyright and license notices. No separate contributor-license agreement is imposed by this draft.
